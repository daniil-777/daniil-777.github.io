## The idea

Creative tools are split by medium: one app for images, another for 3D, another for simulation. ArtPulse puts 2D generation, 3D generation and physics in a single space organised around three verbs.

## Simulate

- **Universal Physics Lab.** Scenarios across mechanics, electricity, optics, thermodynamics, quantum effects and fluids.
- **Hair Physics.** Strand simulation with hard tests such as hurricane wind and a centrifuge spin.
- **Suturing Physics Lab.** Thread, needle and knot physics, including a surgeon's knot.

## Create

- **Architecture Studio.** A parametric tower: change width, taper per floor, terrace depth or twist and the building regenerates.
- **Assembly Studio.** A parts catalog with an exploded view.
- **Image Studio.** Illustration that starts from your own marks.

## Keep

A gallery stores everything the studio produces: images, 3D models, whole worlds and recordings of simulations. 3D assets are generated meshes with level-of-detail variants, Draco-compressed geometry and PBR textures, streamed from a CDN.
